This site publishes two unofficial builds of Blender for Android and the log of
how they are being made. It is a community port. It is **not affiliated with or
endorsed by the Blender Foundation**, and nothing here is an official Blender
release.

## The two branches

The upstream port aims at stability and at running on as many devices as
possible, and ships a full and a lite build. The downstream fork takes that work
and enables as much as it can, tuned and tested on a single device. Neither is
better than the other; they make opposite trades.

## What to expect

Every build here is an alpha. It runs, it renders, and it will have rough edges.
Back up your work.

## Credit

Blender is made by the Blender Foundation and its contributors, and is released
under the GNU GPL. This port only changes how it is built and how it handles
touch input on Android.
