# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS, generated by a Python build script (`tools/build.py`) that uses
the standard library only: no pip dependencies, no venv, no requirements.txt.
Deploy target is GitHub Pages at `blenderandroid.github.io`, which serves the
generated files directly from the repository. The generator owns its own
frontmatter parser and markdown subset renderer.

## Users

Android users who already know Blender and want to run it on a phone or tablet.
They arrive from a Reddit post or a GitHub release link, on the device they
intend to install on, and they are trying to answer two questions fast: will
this run on my device, and which file do I download. A second, smaller audience
follows the porting work itself and returns to read what changed between alphas.

## Product Purpose

Present two unofficial community builds of Blender for Android, make the correct
APK easy to download and verify, and publish the porting progress as an ongoing,
readable record. Success is a visitor installing a build that actually runs on
their device, and returning to read the next update.

## Positioning

One port, two branches, from the same lineage. The upstream fork
(`simfeo/blender`) optimizes for stability and the widest device support, and
ships both a full and a lite build. The downstream fork
(`Wanderson-Magalhaes/blender_for_android`) forks that work and enables as much
as possible, validated on a Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra only. Neither is better;
they are different trades between coverage and capability, and the site exists
to make that trade legible before someone downloads 272 MB on mobile data.

## Operating Context

- Visitors are usually on the target Android device, on mobile data or wifi,
  deciding whether to spend a large download.
- Installation is sideloading: Android will warn about an unknown source, so
  signature and checksum verification is part of the real workflow.
- Releases are irregular and hand-published to GitHub Releases; the site is
  regenerated and committed when one lands.
- Release notes are written in long-form markdown and are the project's primary
  communication channel.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Blender 5.3.0 Alpha. All builds are alpha and explicitly experimental.
- Requirements: arm64 only, Android 12 (API 31) or newer, a Vulkan driver.
  There is no OpenGL fallback.
- Package id `org.blender.blender`.
- Upstream track, `android-alpha-3` (2026-09-07): `blender-full.apk` 272 MB,
  `blender-lite.apk` 193 MB. Alpha 3 dropped three GPU feature requirements
  (dualSrcBlend, shaderClipDistance, vertexPipelineStoresAndAtomics), which
  unblocked ARM Mali devices that previously failed silently at startup.
- Lite omits Cycles and denoising, ffmpeg video, USD, Alembic, OpenVDB,
  MaterialX, LLVM, fluid and smoke simulation, the Ocean modifier, motion
  tracking, the exact and manifold boolean solvers, Draco glTF and PDF export.
  Both builds share interface, add-ons, touch handling, languages and brushes.
- Downstream track, `v5.3.0-alpha.2-android.20260901` (2026-09-01): full build
  only, 232 MB, adds native audio output, tool-variant long press, larger touch
  targets for editor borders and panels.
- Memory: roughly 400 MB on a mid-range tablet, up to ~2 GB on a high-end phone.
  Full peaked near 1.9 GB resident on a Galaxy S22 Ultra.
- The site is fully static. No JavaScript is required for content, downloads,
  or navigation to work.

## Brand Commitments

- The project is **unofficial** and **not affiliated with or endorsed by the
  Blender Foundation**. This must be unmissable and must never be softened.
- The site must not present itself as an official Blender property: no use of
  the official Blender logo as the site's own mark, no `blender.org` framing,
  no "created by the community for the community" official-footer styling.
  The site needs its own mark.
- Voice follows the existing release notes: precise, technical, plainly
  explained, honest about what is broken. No hype, no marketing superlatives.
- User-supplied visual direction: cyberpunk, taken from the neon wet-street
  render the port produced, not from the promotional poster that contained it.
  Dark ground with an orange accent was confirmed separately.

## Product Principles

1. **Device truth before download.** A visitor must know whether their device
   qualifies before a 272 MB transfer starts, not after.
2. **Two branches, no winner.** Present the trade between wide support and
   maximum capability as a genuine choice, never as a ranking.
3. **Unofficial, stated plainly and permanently.** Trust here is built by
   disclaiming, not by borrowing the authority of the Blender Foundation.
4. **The release notes are the product's voice.** The site renders them well
   and does not summarize them into marketing bullets.
5. **Verifiable by default.** Checksums, signing fingerprint, sizes and source
   commits are content, not fine print.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Primary use is a phone in one hand, often outdoors, often on a device the
visitor is unsure is supported. Small viewports and touch targets are the
default case, not the adaptation. Content, downloads, and navigation must work
without JavaScript.
